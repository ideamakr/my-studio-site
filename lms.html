Product · Employee Hub (LMS)

Leave, approvals and who is away, in one place.

We built this for a company of about 100 staff. Balances, requests and approvals live in one system, and approvals follow the reporting lines your company already uses.

Step 1

Each person sees their own leave at a glance.

Pick a staff member to load their balances. Select a card to see the leave taken under that type.

Step 2

Approval levels are set for each client.

Some companies approve leave once, others go up two or three levels. Choose a setup below and send a sample request through it.

Each level is a step the request must pass before the balance is deducted.

Press the button to see the request move through each level.

Step 3

Who is away today, and who is coming up.

Managers see only their own team. Filter by team to check cover before a meeting.

Step 4

A calendar that shows who is away each day.

Dots show how many people are on leave. Select a day to see who, and what type.
