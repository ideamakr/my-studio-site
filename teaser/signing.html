Get forms and documents signed online, with a record of who signed and when.

Send a form or policy to a staff member or client, and track it from sent to signed. No printing, scanning or chasing by message.

Overview

From sent to signed, with no paper

You upload or pick a document and send it to the person who needs to sign. They open it on their phone or computer, read it, and sign with a finger or mouse. You can see each step, and nobody has to print anything.

Every signed document keeps a record of who signed, when, and which version they read, so it can be checked later.

What it will do

  • Send documents and forms for online signature
  • Track each step: sent, viewed, signed
  • A signed record kept for each document
  • Reminders for people who have not signed yet
  • Staff acknowledgements for policies and handbooks

Best for: companies that send policies, contracts, consent forms or onboarding paperwork.
